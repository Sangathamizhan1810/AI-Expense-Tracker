## Objective

Replace all generic "AI Slop" artifacts (glassmorphism blur, neon glow, emoji chips, gradient card surfaces, unaligned figures) with an elite, architectural dark design system and responsive navigation shell tailored for financial command across mobile, tablet, and widescreen viewports.

---

## Architectural Rules (Anti-Slop Contract)

1. **Strict Color Budget (<3% Chroma)**:
   - Ground: `#0c0e12` (pure deep obsidian/slate).
   - Surfaces: `#13171f` (level 1 cards & panels).
   - Raised: `#1a202c` (hover, active controls, modals).
   - Hairlines & Borders: `#222938` (1px hairline contrast, no shadows at rest).
   - Data Signals only: Positive (`#22c55e`), Negative/Overage (`#ef4444`), Warning (`#f59e0b`). Chrome remains strictly ink and neutral.
2. **Typography & Tabular Numerals**:
   - Primary: Geist / Inter (400, 500, 600 weight; no 800+ balloon weights).
   - Monetary & Metrics: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, Geist Mono for currency amounts (`₹1,240.00`) to guarantee column alignment.
3. **Zero-Slop Rule**:
   - Zero emojis in chrome, headings, badges, or buttons. Clean 16px geometric SVG line icons only.
   - Zero backdrop-filter blurs or glowing box-shadows.
   - Zero rounded gradient buttons; solid high-contrast monochrome buttons (`#ffffff` text on `#1a202c` or primary accent `#e2e8f0` with dark ink).

### Task 2: Multi-Device Navigation Shell
- Files: `templates/base.html`, `static/css/style.css`, `static/js/shell.js`
- **Desktop (1025px+)**:
  - Persistent 240px left command rail on page ground.
  - Workspace status (User, Month, Active Budget Health Pill).

### Task 3: Interactive Command Deck (Natural-Language HUD)
- Files: `templates/base.html` or modal component, `static/js/command_bar.js`
